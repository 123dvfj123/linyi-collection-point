group meeting** (≤200 words), the member’s own contribution (≤100 words), and
their contribution to the project, report and presentation. Add these sections
to each logbook before 2026-11-08:

```markdown
## Meeting log
### 2026-10-12 — Week 1 review (18:00–18:30)
- Agenda: ...
- Plans: ...
- Participation: R1 ..., R2 ..., R3 absent ...
- Summary (<=200 words): ...
- My contribution (<=100 words): ...
- Evidence: [minutes](...)

## Report / presentation contribution
| Item | What I wrote, designed or presented | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

## Reflection (100–150 words)
What I learned about software engineering; what I would do differently.
```

Checklist:

- [ ] No placeholder evidence, no “will be filed”, no blank task ID.
- [ ] Each meeting has a minutes link and a personal contribution.
- [ ] Report and presentation contributions are explicit.
- [ ] The reflection is personal, specific and defensible in Q&A.
- [ ] Hours are plausible and consistent with commits/PRs. If an entry is an
      estimate, say so.

R1’s logbook currently records about 85 hours, roughly three times most other
members. That is a project-management risk, not a virtue: redistribute visible
work (slides, report sections, demo) and record “reviewed/merged” separately
from “authored”.

## 6. Q&A preparation

Each speaker should be able to answer these without reading:

1. Why did you choose a hybrid process, and what did you tailor?
2. Show one requirement’s path from a user response to a test.
3. Which requirement did you cut, and who approved it?
4. What did the survey not tell you, and how did you handle that?
5. What was your most important defect, and how did you find it?
6. Why does the pickup code matter, and where is it issued?
7. What is still not implemented, and why is that acceptable for this review?
8. What would you change if you started again?

**Answer rule:** point at the artefact (SRS section, CR number, test name,
commit) rather than giving an opinion. If something is not done, say so and say
why. The rubric rewards a defensible process, not a perfect feature list.
